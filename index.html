About

Pushing methods past their assumptions.

I'm a computer science graduate and current MSc student at Leiden University, based in Den Haag. My work spans classical ML, deep learning research, and edge AI deployment, with a particular interest in the questions that sit at the boundary of what a method can actually do.

I care about methodology: controlled baselines, proper statistical testing, and honest reporting when the results don't go the way you expected. Previously ML intern at Fox Solutions working on Vision Transformers for edge deployment.
